# Masterclass: 90% Cinematic Absorption vs 10% Micro-Interaction

## The Danger of Arcade-Style Distraction
When applying gamification to learning platforms, there is a common pitfall: introducing continuous, reflex-heavy mechanics (the "Subway Surfers" style) in an attempt to keep engagement high.

However, continuous physical reflex actions hijack the brain's executive functioning away from cognitive processing and into procedural motor-response loops. This destroys the fragile conceptual encoding required for deep learning, especially for neurodivergent (ADHD) learners.

## The 90/10 Rule
To maintain peak flow without breaking cognitive absorption:
- **90% of the time**, the learner should be in a state of **Cinematic Absorption**. They watch, listen, and process as rich, 3Blue1Brown-style animations unfold.
- **10% of the time**, at pivotal "aha!" moments, the system introduces a **Zero-Stress Micro-Trigger**.

### Permitted Micro-Triggers
1. `SINGLE_CLICK_IGNITION`: Clicking a visual node to initiate a large chain reaction (e.g., triggering a Base-10 overflow bundle).
2. `EFFORTLESS_DRAG_DROP`: Moving a single conceptual object to a target to see the resulting systemic impact.
3. `SINGLE_KEY_PULSE`: Pressing Space to advance a camera perspective or step through a timeline algorithm, completely decoupled from any timer or failure state.
